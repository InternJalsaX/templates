From Mulberry to Masterpiece

How a true Kanjivaram comes to be

I

Pure Mulberry Silk

Thick, lustrous yarn from Karnataka silk farms, dyed in small batches before weaving.

II

Real Zari

Silver threads coated in gold, woven by hand into borders and pallus.

III

The Korvai Join

Body and border woven separately, then interlocked by hand. It is the mark of a true Kanjivaram.
