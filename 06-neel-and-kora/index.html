From Yarn to You

Four steps, about three weeks, and no shortcuts.

01

Yarn

Silk and zari bought directly from cooperatives, then wound by hand.

02

Dye

Dyed in small batches, so every lot has its own depth of colour.

03

Loom

7 to 21 days on a handloom, depending on the border and motifs.

04

You

Shipped with a card naming the weave, the cluster and the days it took.
