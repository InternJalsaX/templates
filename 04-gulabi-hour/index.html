A note from our founder

It began with a search for the perfect pink.

For my sister's engagement I wanted a rani pink saree that didn't look loud. I visited eleven shops and found it in a weaver's cupboard in Varanasi. Taana is that cupboard, open to everyone: gentle colours, honest silk and help from people who love sarees as much as you do.
