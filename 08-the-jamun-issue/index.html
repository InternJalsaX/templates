Editor's Note

Champagne Hour

Every evening saree in this issue was chosen to move. Tissue that catches a candle, Banarasi that glows under fairy lights, Kanjivaram heavy enough to hold a pleat through a long dinner. We styled them simply: a sleek bun, one statement earring, a blouse with a modern line.

Wear the sindoor Banarasi with a black halter and nothing else. Let the zari do the talking, and keep the pallu loose over one arm so it swings when you walk.

“A saree is the one evening outfit that is different on every woman who wears it.”

The Atelier

Made in Our Studio, Woven Across India

Each season we choose around sixty sarees from weavers in Varanasi and Kanchipuram. Then our studio does the slow part. We match blouses, finish falls and picos by hand, and pre-pleat any saree on request.

Every piece is photographed on the same pedestal under the same light, so the colour you see is the colour you get. If it isn't, we will take it back.
