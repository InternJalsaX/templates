2Design philosophy

The Quiet Drape is the reference store with the volume turned down. Search, the centred logo, the tagged category bar and the product grid all stay where shoppers expect them. Deals stop shouting: there are no red badges, and the ticker is a beige whisper offering a service rather than a discount.

Whitespace, a light Garamond and big daylight photographs do the persuading. The look suits sarees priced ₹6,000 and up, where a shopper wants to feel the fabric through the screen.

Concept 02 of 10 · Brief: Royal Burgundy

Darbar

Rich, luxurious and ceremonial, for weddings and festive silk

The reference storefront in court dress: a burgundy category bar under a cream header, a centred banner with a gold-framed triptych spilling into the page, double-ruled product cards, and dark burgundy bands for best sellers and appointments
Type

Signature

2Design philosophy

Darbar dresses the reference storefront for a wedding. The layout stays familiar: search left, logo centre, a full category bar and a deal grid. Every surface then gets the weight of a trousseau box, with burgundy, antique gold and cream, symmetry, and a double gold rule around anything precious.

The pace is slower than a sale site. Cards carry an always-visible “Add to Bag” and a bridal service ticker in place of a discount. The dark bands give pure silk and real zari room to glow.
